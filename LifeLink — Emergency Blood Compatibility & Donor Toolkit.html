When a family needs blood, minutes decide everything.

Searching for donors by phone and guesswork wastes critical time, and wrong assumptions about compatibility cost lives. LifeLink gives families and volunteers instant, accurate tools to find compatible donors, check who can safely give, and broadcast a clear request.
